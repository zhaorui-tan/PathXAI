For the three matching initial input images, the compact D1 adapter's saved masks and class pixel counts match the historical full-D1 saved outputs exactly. This check covers these three ROIs, not all possible cases. No saved probability arrays were available.

Known report discrepancies remain. The aligned reference describes low-grade adrenocortical carcinoma, no coagulative necrosis, and no vascular or sinusoidal invasion. Both generated reports describe high grade and necrosis and leave invasion unassessed. The historical D1 reports also contained grade and necrosis discrepancies.
  • SO1 says that exact grade is not assessable, but also reports high grade.
  • SO4 reports focal necrosis in one section and says necrosis is not supported by available ROI evidence in another.

The earlier DX1-only tutorial omitted the known site and incorrectly reported liver/hepatocellular carcinoma. The patient-level rerun corrects that input mismatch. Report wording is not byte-identical: Qwen sampling remains stochastic. Reference information was used only for evaluation after generation. The original generated reports and images are preserved, including the discrepancies.

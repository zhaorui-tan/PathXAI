Tumor/Lesion Description:
The submitted tissue sections demonstrate a neoplastic proliferation of adrenocortical cells. The tumor is composed of solid sheets and nests of polygonal cells with abundant eosinophilic to vacuolated (lipid-rich) cytoplasm, consistent with adrenocortical differentiation. The neoplastic cells are separated by fibrous septa. Segmentation analysis indicates a high tumor burden, with neoplastic tissue comprising approximately 98.6% of the sampled regions.
